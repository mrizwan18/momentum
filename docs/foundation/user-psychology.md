Chapter 2: User Psychology & Habit Formation

Why People Quit
Most learners do not quit because they stop caring.
They quit because:
Progress feels invisible.
They don't know the next step.
Missing one day feels like failure.
Sessions feel too long.
There is no emotional reward.
Momentum is designed to solve each of these problems.
---

The Habit Loop
Cue → Open the app at the same time every day.
Craving → See today's mission and an achievable goal.
Action → Complete a focused practice session.
Reward → XP, progress, recordings, achievements and visible growth.
Investment → Add a reflection and recording that increases the value of
tomorrow's session.
---

Emotional Design Goals
The app should help users feel:
Welcomed
Capable
Curious
Consistent
Proud
It should never intentionally make users feel:
Guilty
Ashamed
Overwhelmed
Behind
Inadequate
---

Motivation Hierarchy
Level 1 Show up today.
Level 2 Complete today's mission.
Level 3 Improve one small thing.
Level 4 Notice long-term growth.
Level 5 Become the identity: "I am someone who practices."
---

Identity-Based Design
The app reinforces identity instead of outcomes.
Instead of: "Finish Week 2"
Prefer: "You're becoming someone who practices consistently."
---

The One Thing Principle
Every day there is exactly ONE highest-priority recommendation.
Everything else is secondary.
This reduces cognitive load and decision fatigue.
---

Recovery Philosophy
Missing practice is expected.
Recovery is celebrated.
Recovery sessions are intentionally short and easy to restart momentum.
---

Celebration Ladder
Tiny Win - Checkbox completed
Small Win - Session completed
Medium Win - Weekly assessment passed
Large Win - Chapter completed
Major Win - Campaign completed
Lifetime Win - One year of consistent practice
Each level has increasingly meaningful animations and messaging.
---

Trust Principles
The app never exaggerates.
Predictions are clearly labeled as estimates.
Scores are explainable.
Every recommendation can be justified from user history.
---

Product Promise
Every practice session should answer:
What should I do?
Why does it matter?
How did I improve?
What comes next?
If those four questions are answered consistently, users will naturally
build confidence and long-term habits.
---

Chapter Summary
Momentum is designed around behavioral psychology rather than
motivation.
The objective is not to make users use the app.
The objective is to help users become the kind of person who practices
consistently, even without external motivation.
