Momentum Product Bible
Volume I --- Product
Chapter 3: Jobs To Be Done & Personas
Version 1.0
Purpose
Every feature in Momentum must solve a real user job, not merely provide
functionality.
The question is never:
"What feature should we build?"
The question is:
"What job is the user hiring Momentum to do?"
---

Primary Job To Be Done
"When I want to become better at a difficult skill but struggle to stay
consistent, help me practice regularly, see meaningful progress, and
stay motivated."
---

Supporting Jobs
Tell me exactly what to practice today.
Make it easy to start.
Prove I'm improving.
Help me recover after missing days.
Reduce decision fatigue.
Build confidence over time.
---

Persona 1 --- The Busy Professional
Profile: - Works full-time. - Practices late evenings. - Limited
energy. - Wants structured sessions.
Pain Points: - No time. - Inconsistent schedule. - Feels guilty after
missing days.
Momentum Strategy: - 10--30 minute adaptive sessions. - Recovery mode. -
One clear recommendation. - Resume interrupted sessions.
Success Metric: Practices at least 5 days/week without burnout.
---

Persona 2 --- The Self-Taught Musician
Profile: - Learns from YouTube. - Unsure if improving.
Pain Points: - No feedback. - Random practice. - No roadmap.
Momentum Strategy: - Structured campaign. - Recording timeline. - Growth
reports. - AI Coach.
---

Persona 3 --- The Student
Profile: - High motivation. - Limited discipline.
Pain Points: - Starts enthusiastically. - Stops after weeks.
Momentum Strategy: - Streaks. - XP. - Achievements. - Weekly goals.
---

Persona 4 --- The Hobbyist
Profile: - Wants enjoyable progress. - No professional ambitions.
Pain Points: - Doesn't want pressure.
Momentum Strategy: - Calm UI. - Optional side quests. - Flexible
roadmap.
---

Design Rules
Every feature must answer:
Which persona benefits?
Which job is solved?
How is progress measured?
What emotion should remain after use?
If no clear answer exists, reconsider the feature.
---

Anti-Personas
Do not optimize for:
Competitive leaderboards.
Social comparison.
Addiction mechanics.
Endless notifications.
Momentum is a personal coach, not a social network.
---

Chapter Summary
People don't buy practice trackers.
They buy confidence, progress, and a believable path toward becoming the
person they want to be.
Momentum exists to deliver that transformation through deliberate
practice.
