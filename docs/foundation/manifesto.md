Vision
Momentum is an offline-first platform for deliberate practice.
It is not a course platform.
It is not a habit tracker.
It is not a note taking app.
Momentum exists for one purpose:

> Help people become exceptional at difficult skills through consistent,
> measurable practice.
> The first Skill Pack is Riyaaz (Vocals).
> Future Skill Packs include Guitar, Piano, Coding, Languages and Fitness.

---

The Problem
People rarely fail because they lack talent.
They fail because:
They don't know what to practice.
They lose consistency.
Progress feels invisible.
Missing a few days destroys motivation.
Existing apps teach information rather than building habits.
Momentum solves this by making showing up every day the primary
product goal.
---

Product Philosophy
Principle 1
Consistency beats intensity.
Ten minutes every day is more valuable than three hours once a week.
---

Principle 2
Effort deserves celebration.
The product rewards:
showing up
recording yourself
reflecting
recovering after breaks
---not innate talent.
---

Principle 3
Evidence beats motivation.
Users should hear, see and measure improvement.
Motivation naturally follows visible progress.
---

Principle 4
Recovery is part of mastery.
Missing practice is expected.
The platform never punishes.
Instead it gently guides users back into momentum.
---

The Emotional Journey
Every session should create this sequence:
Open app ↓
Feel welcomed
↓
Understand today's mission
↓
Practice without friction
↓
Finish successfully
↓
See measurable improvement
↓
Feel excited to return tomorrow
---

North Star
The app succeeds when users say:
"I can't believe how much better I sound compared to three months ago."
---not---
"I have a 100-day streak."
---

The Skill Engine
Momentum is powered by a generic Skill Engine.
Every skill supplies:
Campaign
Curriculum
Exercises
Assessments
Coach Rules
Recommendation Rules
Progress Rules
The core platform remains unchanged.
---

Riyaaz Skill Pack
Purpose:
Help beginners become confident singers through structured daily
practice.
Core outcomes:
Better breath control
Better pitch
Better rhythm
Better confidence
Better consistency
The roadmap is six months long but adaptive.
---

Non-Negotiable Product Values
Offline-first.
Local-first ownership of data.
Beautiful minimal UI.
One primary action per screen.
Fast enough to feel instant.
Calm over clutter.
Progress always visible.
Never shame the user.
Encourage deliberate practice.
Build for years, not weeks.
---

Success Metrics
Primary: - Weekly Active Practitioners - Average Practice Days / Week -
90-day retention - Roadmap completion
Secondary: - Recording frequency - Reflection completion - Recovery
success rate - Growth score improvement
